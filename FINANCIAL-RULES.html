Financial rules for imported activity

The recurring budget is a projection; observed spending is a separate, incomplete estimate and is not subtracted from the recurring projection. The app never adds a payment between people or a bank transfer as a new expense. A bank payment is cash outflow, a Splitwise paid share is what the user fronted, and an owed share is their own consumption; a settlement changes a debt, not the expense again. Splitwise balances are displayed as supplied because their sign has not been independently established.

Bank purchase notices that are pending or confirmed are included provisionally; discarded notices, zero amounts and transfers are excluded. Splitwise deleted records and payments are excluded. An unpaid personal share can be included when there is no same-day, same-merchant bank notice suggesting overlap. An unmatched expense that the user paid is excluded and counted as ambiguous, because adding both sources could double-count. Amounts with missing shares, negative values (including refunds), unsupported precision or unsupported Splitwise currency are omitted and counted; they are not guessed. A changed or deleted Splitwise record is recalculated on the next snapshot, and missing provider rows are retained rather than inferred deleted.

An automatic possible pair requires equal calendar date as stored, normalized exact merchant, equal currency and bank amount equal to the user's Splitwise paid share, with a unique candidate on each side. UYU and USD are supported for Splitwise matching. For that tentative pair the bank amount is replaced by the owed share, without claiming independent confirmation. The dashboard exposes each match and allows rejecting it; rejected pairs can be restored and are persisted across syncs. Duplicate amounts or merchants, installments, ambiguous pairs, late dates, currency conversions, unsupported values and partial payments that do not meet the rule are not guessed. The estimate can be incomplete and requires review when ambiguity is reported.

Totals are shown separately in original currency; no exchange rate is invented. Calendar matching and month selection use the stored ISO timestamp's date prefix, which can differ from local time around midnight. Classification uses saved merchant rules and remains editable on the source screens. An edited category never changes the monetary match. These rules do not establish whether a bank notice is authentic or a payment settled.
